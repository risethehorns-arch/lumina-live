Why this route

Buying a finished flat is the last chance to influence it.

Coming in while a building is still going up is not only about price. It is the only point at which the layout, the finish and the delivery date are still questions rather than facts.

01 — Access

Before it is published

The contractor brings each building to us before it goes anywhere else. Nothing about an owner, a price or a floor leaves the mandate without consent — the same rule that governs the rest of the firm.

02 — Specification

Choose while it is still choosable

Come in early enough and the joinery, the stone, the kitchen and often the internal layout are still open. Come in at handover and you are negotiating over someone else's decisions.

03 — Accountability

One name on the drawings

Every question about structure, services, tolerances or snagging goes to the same engineer, and comes back from the same engineer. There is no chain of subcontractors to lose an answer in.

04 — Scale

A floor, or the whole thing

These are not only single units. A whole floor, a block of floors, or an entire building can be taken — which is a different conversation, and one we would rather have early than late.

Our role

We market these buildings. We do not own them.

Worth saying plainly, because it changes what our advice is worth. The contractor builds and owns; we introduce, advise and negotiate; and we are paid a commission on completion that is agreed before you commit to anything.

How it works

Four stages, and the price of entry rises at each one.

Where a building sits on this line is the single thing that decides what is still negotiable. Ask us where the current ones are.

  1. Stage 01

    Land

    Plot secured, setting out agreed, permits lodged.

  2. Stage 02

    Structure

    Excavation, raft, columns and slabs. The floors exist, and the building is now on the shell.

  3. Stage 03

    Finishing

    Locally tashteeb, and the whole of it is one price. Three phases, quoted and paid for together — and the only stretch where what you get is still a question rather than a fact.

    Handover

    Snagging, registration, utilities and keys.
