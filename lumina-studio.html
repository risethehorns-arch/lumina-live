What is included

Three things, and they are usually one job.

You can take one. Most firms need the middle one and discover they needed the other two — a site with nothing to say and a feed with nothing to point at are the same problem twice.

01

The website

Not a brochure with a contact form. A place your listings actually live, that loads on a phone in a weak signal, and that you can update without ringing anybody.

  • Designed and built from nothing — no template, no page builder
  • Listings driven by a spreadsheet your office already keeps
  • Every property with its own link, ready to send
  • Enquiries land in WhatsApp, where your clients already are
  • Yours outright: the files, the domain, the hosting account
02

The social blueprint

Not us posting for you. A written system your own team can run — what to shoot, how to frame it, what to say underneath, and what to stop doing.

  • A shooting guide for listings: angles, light, order, what to skip
  • Post formats that suit property rather than fashion
  • Templates in your typefaces and colours, not a stock pack
  • A weekly rhythm a two-person office can actually sustain
  • How a post gets someone to a listing page instead of a dead end
03

The brand

The part that decides whether a stranger takes you seriously before you have said a word. Built to survive a phone screen, a signboard and a printed particulars sheet.

  • Wordmark and marks, drawn rather than picked from a library
  • Typefaces and a colour set, with the reasoning written down
  • How a listing card, a sign and a document each look
  • A short book your team can follow without asking us
  • Every file in a format you can hand to anybody later

How it works

Four stages, and you can stop after any of them.

Nothing here requires a twelve-month contract. Each stage produces something you keep whether or not you carry on.

i

A conversation, and a look at what you have

What you sell, who to, and where it is going wrong. We go through your current site and your last month of posts and tell you what we would change first — that part costs nothing and is useful even if you stop there.

ii

A written proposal, with the price on it

Scope, stages, what you provide, what we provide, and the fee. One document, one number, agreed before anything starts. No discovery phase that quietly bills for itself.

iii

Building, in the open

You see it on a live link from the first week, not at a reveal. Changes are cheap early and expensive late, and a client who watches it happen catches the expensive ones while they are still cheap.

iv

Handover, meaning handover

The files, the accounts, the domain and a written note on how to run it — in your name, not ours. If you never speak to us again the thing keeps working, which is the only honest test of whether it was built properly.
