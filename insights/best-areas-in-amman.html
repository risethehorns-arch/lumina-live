Choosing an area in Amman.

Seven districts, what each one is good at, and roughly what it costs. Written against the properties we currently hold rather than the market in general.

West Amman is not one market. The districts below sit within a few kilometres of each other and behave quite differently — on price, on noise, on how long a good property stays available. This is a plain comparison of the seven we work in, with the ranges taken from our own current stock.

The seven, briefly

  • Abdoun — 68 residences with us, the largest share by some margin. Embassies, mature streets, and almost all of the villa stock. Annual rents from about 8,000 to 120,000 JOD, typically near 15,000.
  • The Circles (1st–5th) — 14 residences, and the only part of the portfolio where buying is a real option rather than an exception (4 of 14). Rents spread widely, roughly 7,000 to 70,000 JOD.
  • Swefieh — 10 apartments. The walkable choice: shops, restaurants and schools close by, at around 17,500 JOD a year for a typical flat.
  • Shmesani — 9 apartments, close to the hospitals and offices. Typically 13,000 JOD a year, and usually the shortest commute of the seven.
  • Jabal Al Weibdeh — 7 residences. Older buildings, galleries and cafés, smaller floor plates. The most characterful district and the least predictable stock.
  • Um Uthaina — 6 apartments, quiet and central, sitting between Swefieh's bustle and Abdoun's calm.
  • Dair Ghbar — 4 residences, a small pocket that includes some of the largest floor areas we handle.

If you are early in the search, pick the district before the property. Moving between districts changes what your budget buys far more than moving between buildings inside one.

A rough way to narrow it down

If you want space and quiet, start with Abdoun and Dair Ghbar. That is where the villas and the larger floor plates are, and where you are least likely to hear the street.

If you want to walk to things, look at Swefieh first, then Jabal Al Weibdeh. Both put daily life within a few minutes on foot, which matters more than most people expect once they have tried the alternative.

If you are buying rather than renting, the Circles are the place to begin. Most of what we hold elsewhere is rental, and sale instructions in Abdoun tend to be introduced privately rather than listed.

If the commute decides it, Shmesani sits closest to the hospital and office cluster, and Um Uthaina is the reasonable compromise between that and the quieter hills.

What a listing does not tell you

Two flats in the same building, same size, same rent, can be very different homes. Which street the entrance is on, which way the bedrooms face, whether there is a designated parking space, and how the block handles water and heating in February are all worth asking before you spend an afternoon viewing.

Many Amman buildings are cut into a slope, so a floor described as −1 may be full height with a garden behind it. Do not rule out a level on the label alone.

Availability moves quickly at the sensible end of the market. We confirm with the owner before arranging anything, which is slower but means you are not shown something that went last week.
