The approach

Most of Amman's best addresses never reach a listing portal.

Lumina works the other way around. We hold the relationships with the owners, the developers and the embassies — so the shortlist arrives before the market does.

i.

Discretion by default

Owners come to us because nothing is published without consent. Names, addresses and figures stay inside the mandate — for sellers and buyers alike.

ii.

Fluent with diplomats

Residency paperwork, embassy lease clauses, notarised translation, utility transfers. We run the administrative side in-house, in three languages.

iii.

One side of the table

We represent you, not the transaction. Where a valuation doesn't hold up, we say so before you view — and we have talked clients out of buildings before.
