For owners

Property management

Your property run from here while you are somewhere else. Tenants found and checked, rent and dues collected, repairs arranged and supervised, and the place inspected four times a year whether or not anything is wrong.

  • One named contact here, not a department
  • A statement every month, a condition report every quarter
  • Nothing is spent without your yes

Property evaluation

A written opinion of what a property is worth, with the evidence attached. Before you list, before you offer, or because someone has already given you a number and you would like a second one.

  • Yours to show anyone — no obligation to sell with us
