What happens, and when

Four inspections a year you did not have to ask for.

The property is inspected quarterly whether or not anything is wrong, and the condition report reaches you either way. It is the difference between finding out about a leak and being told about one.

  • Quarterly inspection, photographed, with a written condition note.
  • A monthly statement: rent in, costs out, and what is outstanding.
  • Anything urgent reaches you the same day, in the time zone you are in.

How it works

Three rules we run everything by.

i

One name, not a department

The person who inspected your property is the person who answers about it. Nobody on our side has to read a file before they can tell you what the kitchen looks like, because they were standing in it in March.

ii

Nothing is spent without a yes

Repairs are quoted before they are ordered and invoiced at what they cost — there is no margin added to a plumber. An agreed standing limit for small items is the only exception, and you set it.

iii

The report goes out even when it is dull

A month with nothing in it still produces a statement. Management that only contacts you when something is wrong trains you to dread the message, and it hides the months where something was quietly going wrong.
