The people

Seven people, one desk.

Lumina is small on purpose. A residence here is not passed between departments — it is held by one advisor from the first message to the keys, and the same number reaches every one of us.

How we work.

Most agencies route an enquiry through whoever is free. We do the opposite, because the useful thing about a small firm is that the person who answers has already stood in the property.

01

One advisor, start to finish

Whoever introduces a residence stays with it — through the viewing, the negotiation and the handover. You are not passed to a colleague halfway through, and you never explain yourself twice.

02

One number

+962 77 150 5250 reaches the whole desk on WhatsApp. Message it and the advisor who knows that property answers — not a switchboard, and not a form that arrives somewhere three days later.

03

Confirmed before we show it

Availability and the final figure are checked with the owner before a viewing is arranged. It is the reason we sometimes tell you a listing is already gone instead of showing it to you anyway.
